## Cold-eye boundary

The differentiator is not that five signatures discover Uruguay's “weakness.”
Five classifiable defensive exposures cannot justify that claim. The
evidence-backed value is:

`opponent repeated sequences → manager-team prior exposure → choose two review
questions → lock → reveal an unselected matchup pattern → inspect the source
scene → revise the next agenda`

The product may describe named-team recorded sequences, observation gaps,
event-associated players, source receipts, and later shot records. It must not
claim player intent, marking, physical contact, causal training effects, an
optimal tactic, reinforcement learning, or AI recommendation.
