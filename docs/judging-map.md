## Cold-eye claim boundary

The differentiator is not that `5/4/1` predicts `5/2/3`, and a close count is
not treated as proof that training was correct. The evidence-backed value is the
manager workflow:

`separate small samples → allocate scarce preparation → precommit → reveal a
contradicting match → inspect source events → decide what to revisit`.

The product may describe:

- Portugal's group-stage `14/14` classifiable corner situations;
- Uruguay's group-stage `5/6` classifiable defensive situations;
- the held-out Uruguay–Portugal `5/2/3` situations;
- player-linked recorded event chains and four shots within ten seconds;
- the allocation-to-observation differences and the manager's next-meeting memo.

It must not claim:

- that a player received, won, marked, or could reach the ball unless the source
  records that relation;
- that the allocation prevented a shot, improved defense, or changed the result;
- that reinforcement learning, AI, or an optimizer selected the plan;
- that the three categories are the optimal tactical representation.

Data credibility, accessibility, visual clarity, reliability, and demo quality
support the official criteria rather than forming an invented scorecard.
