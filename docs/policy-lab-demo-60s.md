## First-five-second contract

At desktop and `390×844`, the first frame must make visible or immediately
reachable without scrolling:

- `16강 전날, 포르투갈 코너 영상은 무엇부터 볼까요?`;
- `키커 콰레스마 · 첫 기록에 등장한 선수 게헤이루`;
- `3장면 · 2경기` and the no-pass-target/no-first-contact boundary;
- direct three-axis versus adjacent comparison support;
- the three-step path `선수·이벤트 연결 → 두 안건 선택 → 가려 둔 맞대결`;
- `먼저 돌려볼 영상 검토 안건 두 개`;
- the first question-selection control; and
- the small-sample observation-gap boundary.

The detailed planning correction and research metrics stay outside the narrated
60 seconds. Every editorial overlay is labelled `[편집 요약]`; no generated
match footage or simulated outcome is used.

## Claim boundary

The narration may say `첫 기록`, `직접 비교`, `참고 장면`,
`기록`, `장면`, `두 안건`, `결과 전에 잠금`, `선택 밖`, and
`다음 회의`. It must not call a recorded actor the receiver or first contact,
describe any count as a team trait, score the manager, rank the two questions,
attribute a later shot to the selection, or imply a preferred response. `0`
means only that a direct comparison receipt was not found in the small admitted
sample.
